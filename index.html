01 — About

Who's Behind Neuroforge

Full-stack development, approached as a craft — not just a checklist of technologies.

I'm Ibrahim Owolabi — a full-stack developer working under the name Neuroforge, based in Ibadan, Nigeria.

I build the full stack of a product: the interface someone actually uses, the API and business logic behind it, and the database that holds it together — then I take it all the way to a live, production deployment. I didn't come up through a bootcamp cohort or a CS degree; I came up by building things repeatedly until they worked, which is a slower path but one that leaves you comfortable owning a project end to end rather than one slice of it.

My approach is straightforward: understand the problem before writing code, keep the architecture simple enough to explain, and ship something that actually works in production — not just on localhost.

I work across business and marketing websites, full-stack web applications, SaaS platforms and dashboards, e-commerce and marketplace flows, backend/API systems, and AI-integrated applications. I take on client work — like the site I built for Imperial Shelter-Tech Konsult — and I build my own products, like Veyra and Amoura, both currently in active development.
