01 — About

The Person Behind The Forge

Self-taught, self-driven, and obsessed with the craft of building things that work — and feel good to use.

I'm Ibrahim Owolabi — a full-stack developer and creative technologist working under the name Neuroforge, based in Ibadan, Nigeria.

My path into tech didn't start in a classroom — it started in a bedroom, fuelled by curiosity and late nights, with one question: what if I could build anything I imagined? That question led through HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and out the other side into someone who ships real products for real clients.

Under Neuroforge, I've delivered everything from real-time chat applications and SaaS dashboards to AI-integrated tools and backend systems that hold up under load. I work at the seam between engineering and design — where a clean API meets an interface someone actually enjoys clicking through.

"I don't just build websites. I build things people interact with, feel something in, and remember after they've closed the tab."

My stack spans React, Next.js, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, and PostgreSQL, paired with motion tools like GSAP, Three.js, and WebGL. I'm as comfortable in Figma at midnight as I am pushing a clean commit to production at dawn.
