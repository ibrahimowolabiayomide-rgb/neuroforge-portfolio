The problem

I wanted a deliberately small, private place to keep letters, memories and music together. Not another social app competing for attention.

Three rooms, one space

The design direction. It describes the intent, not a finished feature list.

  1. 01LettersDirection
  2. 02MemoriesDirection
  3. 03MusicDirection

What I built

I am designing and building it on my own, from the visual design through to the deployment.

  • Next.js, deployed on Vercel
  • A private, personal space for letters and memories
  • A polished visual design

Decisions and reasons

  1. Keeping the scope narrow

    For something this personal, restraint is part of the design. I would rather leave a feature out than add capability for its own sake, so the first versions focus on a few things done well.

  2. Music as part of the same space

    Music sits alongside letters and memories as part of one experience instead of being a separate feature.
