The problem

The consultancy had completed installation work but little online to show for it. Visitors could not see past projects, understand the range of services, or make contact without already having a phone number.

What I built

I designed and built the site and its content system, and prepared it for hand-over. That covered service pages for the six core offerings, a portfolio of completed projects, and the ways to ask for a quote.

  • Rigid panel mesh fencing, solar-powered electric fencing and perimeter security systems
  • Architectural design and construction, land surveying and CCTV installation
  • A project portfolio and company pages driven by the content system
  • A contact route and a WhatsApp quote flow
  • Sanity content wired into the home page, the About page, projects and blog

Decisions and reasons

  1. Content lives in Sanity

    Services, projects and posts come from Sanity, so the client's content is edited in a Studio instead of in code. I also reworked the Studio dashboard so it is usable on small screens.

  2. Quote requests start where customers already are

    Rather than build another lead form and a place to store its entries, quote requests go straight to WhatsApp, with a scannable QR code for visitors on a computer. It is the channel the client's customers already use.

  3. A server-side contact route

    The contact form posts to a Next.js route that sends email through Resend. An earlier version relied on a hosted form service. I replaced it so validation, delivery and failure handling are in my control. A production readiness audit then found missing security headers and no rate limiting on that route, and I fixed both.
